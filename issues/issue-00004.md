Reported by the developer (2026-10-20): the menu bar item frequently disappears while the app is running, and the notch can hide it.

**Cause (measured on macOS 26.6, built-in display 1728 pt wide, notch x 771–956):**
- Status items are laid out leftwards from Control Center; the most recently added goes furthest left. On a notched display only the area right of the notch holds status items. Anything that would fall under or left of it is not drawn, with no error.
- ClaudeMon's item shows text, so it is 74–90 pt wide and its width changes on every refresh. Before the fix it sat at x 918, partly under the notch.
- Launched after other menu bar apps (reproduced with a helper adding 6–12 items), it was placed at x 37–502, left of the notch, and never drawn.
- SwiftUI `MenuBarExtra` exposes no `NSStatusItem`, so the app could neither detect this nor recover from it.

**Spec impact:** the requested fix shows a Dock icon, which contradicts DEC-1, F-9.AC-1 and R-CMUI-001 ("menu bar only, no Dock icon"). It needs a definition change request (F-9.CR-1, superseding DEC-1) before it can be accepted.
